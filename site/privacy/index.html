Food omniScan

Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

Food omniScan ("we") helps you understand packaged food: you scan a barcode or photograph a label and we explain the ingredients, nutrition, allergens and recalls. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Android and iOS apps and the website.

What we collect

  • Account details: email address, display name, country and a securely hashed password.
  • Eater profiles you create: names, allergies, diet preferences, ingredients to avoid and goals. You choose whether to store these; they are used only to personalise results.
  • What you do in the app: products you scan or save, shopping lists, pantry, budget, food diary, water and weight entries, recipes, meal plans and product reviews.
  • Photos you choose to send: label, product or meal photos you ask the AI to read. Barcode scanning and on-device text reading happen on your phone and are not uploaded.
  • Questions you ask the assistant, so it can answer them.
  • Subscription status: your plan and renewal date. Payments are handled by Google Play, the App Store or Stripe; we never see or store card numbers.
  • Basic technical data: IP address and request logs kept for security and abuse prevention.

We do not use advertising identifiers, sell personal data, or show third-party ads.

How we use it

  • To provide the service: scores, allergen and diet checks, alerts, diary and planning features.
  • To answer AI requests. Questions and photos are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate an answer and are not used by us to train models.
  • To manage your subscription and enforce plan allowances.
  • To send the notifications you switch on (recalls, expiry reminders, weekly reports).
  • To keep the service secure, moderate reported reviews and fix problems.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers that run parts of the service for us: our cloud hosting and database provider, Google (Gemini AI), and the payment providers you use (Google Play, Apple, Stripe, Paystack, RevenueCat for app-store receipts). Product lookups send only the barcode to Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central. Product reviews you post are shown to other users without your name. We share data with authorities only when the law requires it.

Sensitive information

Allergies and diet preferences can reveal health information. We store them only with your consent, use them only to check foods for you, and you can remove them at any time. Food omniScan provides general nutrition information and is not a medical device or medical advice.

Your choices and rights

  • Export all your data from Profile, Privacy & data.
  • Delete your account in the app (Profile, Privacy & data, Delete account) or as described on our account deletion page.
  • Notifications can be switched off in the app settings.
  • Report or block: you can report any review and block its author from the review's menu.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, California or Nigeria), you may also have rights to access, correct or restrict use of your data. Contact us to use them.

Retention and security

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, your profile, diary, lists and other personal data are deleted immediately, and any backup copies are overwritten within 30 days. Product photos you submitted that moderators approved stay as anonymous product data. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and passwords are hashed.

Children

Accounts are for people aged 13 and over. A parent can create eater profiles for children within their own account.
