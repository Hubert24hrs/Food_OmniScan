Delete your Food omniScan account

You can delete your account and data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Food omniScan and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Privacy & data.
  3. Tap Delete my account, type DELETE and confirm.

Without the app

Email iheanachohubert@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my Food omniScan account". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account, email and password.
  • Eater profiles, allergies, diet preferences and avoid lists.
  • Scan history, saved products, food diary, water and weight logs, shopping lists, pantry, budget, recipes, meal plans and reviews.
  • Assistant conversations and photos you sent for AI reading.

Kept: product photos and corrections that moderators already approved stay as anonymous product data (not linked to you), and payment records that tax law requires us to keep. Any backup copies are overwritten within 30 days.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription: cancel it in the store first.
